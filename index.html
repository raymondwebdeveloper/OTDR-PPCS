Training Programs

Skills Built for the Real Field.

Develop practical fiber optic skills through technical instruction, equipment demonstrations, hands-on exercises and field-oriented training.

01 / COURSE ↗

Fiber Optic Fundamentals

Learn the principles of fiber optics including fiber construction, transmission, components, connectors and network fundamentals.

02 / COURSE ↗

Fusion Splicing

Hands-on training covering fiber preparation, cleaving, fusion splicing and proper splice protection techniques.

03 / COURSE ↗

OTDR Testing

Understand OTDR traces, events, distance, attenuation and basic fiber link analysis.

04 / COURSE ↗

Optical Power Measurement

Learn how optical power meters and related testing equipment are used to measure fiber optic performance.

05 / COURSE ↗

Fiber Installation

Practical training focused on installation methods, cable handling, routing and field practices.

06 / COURSE ↗

Fiber Troubleshooting

Learn systematic approaches for identifying faults and understanding common fiber network problems.

Training Philosophy Learn the technology. Practice the skill. Understand the field.
